## Timed sequence

The slots total **270 seconds** and are **time budgets, not instructions to wait**. As soon as the first draft is saved and the assistant finishes its turn, speak the correction immediately, even if that is before 1:25. Do not pause, mute the microphone, or narrate to the audience between draft creation and correction. During the active call, speak only to the assistant; audience narration could be interpreted as a new request. After the corrected draft is saved, say “All good” and end the call before the extended record review. Spend any spare time on that off-call review. Allow tools to finish; if the live flow cannot finish honestly, use the fallback below rather than narrating an expected result as observed.

## Honest fallback if voice fails

Keep any saved draft and the session intact. End the call, or use the displayed recovery control, before speaking to the audience; wait for the card to unlock before editing or approving. Then say: “The live voice connection did not complete in this take. I’ll show the form workflow, and label it as a form demonstration.” If recovery cannot complete, stop the approval portion and show the error; do not bypass it.

If no draft was saved, enter the same still-fresh property, unit, general trade, and issue in the repair form, then use **Check repair records** and **Save draft**. If a draft was saved, continue that draft. Edit access to “After 3 pm, please knock,” keep Requested date empty, save, review, and approve only if the card permits it. Label this as form editing, not spoken correction. **Text conversation** also does not establish microphone or same-call speech success. A fallback take may illustrate recovery, but it does not satisfy the planned live voice demonstration; a successful voice take remains pending.
